Aquatic Weed Removal Built for Shallow Water

Aquatic Weed Harvesting LLC provides mechanical removal of aquatic vegetation, muck, shoreline material, and debris from lakes and ponds across New York, New Jersey, and Pennsylvania.

Based in the Hudson Valley, we help homeowners, lake associations, commercial properties, and municipalities restore access to overgrown water without applying aquatic herbicides.

Our Focus

Lake and Pond Cleanup Made for Difficult Water

Many aquatic weed problems develop in shallow coves, around docks, along shorelines, and in smaller waterways where full-size harvesters may not have enough room or depth to operate.

Our agile workboats have an 8-inch shallow draft, allowing us to reach vegetation in shallow and confined areas. Their design also provides greater flexibility when working around waterfront structures and in changing surface conditions.

We assess each waterbody individually and select the equipment based on the vegetation, debris, sediment, depth, and property access.

Equipment Capabilities

Designed to Reach the Weeds

Many aquatic weed problems develop in shallow areas around docks, shorelines, coves, and pond edges. Our workboats have an 8-inch shallow draft, allowing them to reach areas where larger harvesting equipment may not have sufficient depth or operating space.

  • 8-Inch Shallow DraftProvides access to shallow water where excessive growth often collects.
  • 5-to-6-Foot Cutting DepthAllows submerged vegetation to be cut well below the water’s surface.
  • Five Quick-Change AttachmentsThe cutter, vegetation bucket, skimmer bucket, root rake, and hydraulic pole saw allow the equipment to adapt to different vegetation and debris.
  • Two-Boat CapabilityTwo workboats can be deployed when appropriate for the project’s size and conditions.

Permits

Guidance Through the Permitting Process

Permit requirements vary by state, waterbody, plant species, and project type.

Aquatic Weed Harvesting LLC does not issue permits, but we can help customers identify the appropriate agency or contact. We are also available to answer project-related questions during the permitting process when needed.

Customers remain responsible for obtaining any required approvals before work begins.
