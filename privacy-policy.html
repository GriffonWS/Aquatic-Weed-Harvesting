Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Aquatic Weed Harvesting LLC (“Aquatic Weed Harvesting,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, submit a contact form, request a quote, or otherwise communicate with us.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

Information You Provide

When you contact us or request a quote, you may provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing or service address
  • Property, lake, pond, or project location
  • Information about the services you are requesting
  • Details included in messages, forms, or other communications
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

Please do not submit sensitive personal information through our website unless it is necessary for your request.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, our service providers and we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, including:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Pages viewed and links clicked
  • Date and time of visits
  • Approximate geographic location
  • Website interaction and traffic information
  • Cookie and similar technology data

2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to questions, contact requests, and quote requests
  • Evaluate potential projects and provide requested services
  • Communicate with customers and prospective customers
  • Schedule, manage, and perform services
  • Operate, maintain, and improve our website
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Personalize and improve the user experience
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website and business
  • Detect or prevent fraud, misuse, and unlawful activity
  • Maintain business and transaction records
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims
  • Support other legitimate business purposes disclosed when information is collected

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags, and similar technologies. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help websites function, remember preferences, and understand visitor activity.

We may use:

  • Essential cookies to support website functionality and security
  • Analytics cookies to measure traffic and understand how visitors interact with the website
  • Preference cookies to remember selections or settings
  • Third-party cookies provided by analytics, hosting, or other service providers

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect certain website features or functionality.

Where required by law, we will request consent before using nonessential cookies.

4. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or similar analytics services to collect and analyze information about website traffic and usage. Google Analytics may collect information such as your IP address, device information, browser type, pages visited, and interactions with the website.

Google may process this information according to its own privacy policies. You can learn more about Google’s privacy practices at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google also provides an Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on at: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

5. How We Share Information

We may share information with third parties when reasonably necessary for website operations, analytics, service delivery, and legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Website hosting and maintenance providers
  • Website developers and technology vendors
  • Analytics providers
  • Email, communications, and customer-management providers
  • Professional advisers, such as attorneys, accountants, and insurers
  • Contractors, vendors, or service providers involved in responding to or fulfilling a service request
  • Government agencies, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when required by law

We may also disclose information:

  • To comply with a law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or other legal process
  • To protect our rights, property, safety, customers, or others
  • To investigate or prevent suspected fraud, security incidents, misuse, or unlawful conduct
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale of assets, or similar business transaction
  • With your consent or at your direction

We do not sell personal information for money. We also do not knowingly share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. If our practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide any notices or choices required by applicable law.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to inquiries and quote requests
  • Provide and manage services
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy
  • Comply with legal, tax, accounting, insurance, or regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Protect our legal and business interests

Retention periods may vary based on the type of information, the reason it was collected, and applicable legal requirements. We may retain aggregated or de-identified information when it can no longer reasonably identify an individual.

7. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by us.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Request information about the categories of personal information we collect and disclose
  • Obtain a portable copy of certain personal information
  • Object to or request restrictions on certain processing
  • Withdraw consent when processing is based on consent
  • Opt out of certain sales, sharing, targeted advertising, or profiling activities, if applicable
  • Appeal a decision concerning a privacy request
  • Receive equal service without unlawful discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. An authorized agent may submit a request when permitted by applicable law, but we may require proof of authorization and identity verification.

Aquatic Weed Harvesting LLC does not currently engage in activities that would require all of these rights to apply in every jurisdiction. This section does not grant rights beyond those provided by law.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the information in the “Contact Us” section below.

Where legally required, we will recognize applicable browser-based opt-out preference signals.

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by third parties. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content practices of those third parties.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information through our website may contact us using the information below.

Aquatic Weed Harvesting LLC is based in the United States. If you access our website from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate.

Privacy and data-protection laws in these locations may differ from the laws in your country.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, services, technology, or legal obligations.

When we make changes, we will post the updated policy on this website and revise the effective date at the top. Your continued use of the website after an updated policy is posted is subject to the revised policy.
