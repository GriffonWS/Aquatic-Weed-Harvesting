Lake Weed Removal

We remove aquatic weeds, cattails, algae, branches, and floating debris using a specialized workboat designed for shallow, hard-to-access waters. Our professional lake weed cutter service ensures prompt and effective clearance of overgrown aquatic vegetation.

No herbicide application. Just mechanical cutting, collection, and removal.

  • Designed for lakes, ponds, and shallow waterways
  • Multiple attachments for different removal needs
  • Collected vegetation removed from the water

What we clear

Complete aquatic cleaning solutions for any waterfront.

Aquatic growth can block docks, crowd swimming areas, restrict boat access, and overwhelm shallow shorelines. Our adaptable workboat uses specialized attachments for lake swim area clearing and to address any vegetation or debris affecting your property.

  • 01

    Cattail and rooted vegetation removal

    Heavy cattail growth and rooted vegetation can quickly take over shallow water and shorelines. The root-rake attachment lifts and removes dense plant material that cannot be managed with a cutter alone.

    • Cattail removal
    • Root-rake service
    • Designed for shallow shoreline growth
  • 02

    Algae and floating material removal

    Remove algae and smaller floating particles from the water's surface. The skimmer attachment collects fine material that the underwater cutter or root rake cannot handle effectively.

    • Surface algae removal
    • Collection of fine floating material
    • Suitable for ponds, coves, and shoreline areas
  • 03

    Branch, tree, and log removal

    Low-hanging branches, small trees, and downed logs can obstruct waterfront access and complicate shoreline maintenance. The hydraulic pole saw allows us to cut and manage woody material while working from the water.

    • Low-hanging branch removal
    • Small tree and downed log cutting
    • Work completed from the water side
  • 04

    Invasive aquatic weed removal

    Target aggressive aquatic plants before they spread further through the water body. We adapt the removal method to the species, growth pattern, and conditions present.

    • Eurasian watermilfoil
    • Water chestnut
    • Hydrilla

The equipment

Built for shallow and difficult water.

Large aquatic harvesters need sufficient depth, open water, and suitable launch access. Many waterfront problems occur under opposite conditions.

Our compact work boat is designed to operate in smaller, shallower waterways and can remain effective in windy conditions that may limit larger harvesting equipment.

  • 03

The process

A straightforward removal process.

  1. 1

    Show us the problem

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What types of water bodies do you work in?

We work in lakes, ponds, and smaller, shallow waterways. Access and equipment suitability are reviewed before each project.

Do you use aquatic herbicides?

No. The services described on this page use mechanical equipment to cut, lift, collect, or remove unwanted material.

Can you work in shallow water?

The work boat is designed for small and shallow waterways where larger aquatic harvesters may have difficulty operating.

What kinds of weeds do you remove?

Common vegetation includes Eurasian watermilfoil, water chestnut, hydrilla, lily pads, cattails, and algae.

Can you remove branches and downed trees?

The hydraulic pole saw can be used for low-hanging branches, small trees, and downed logs in or near the water.
