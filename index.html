What We Pull Out of Your Water

Eurasian Watermilfoil

Dense, feathery vegetation that propagates easily from small floating fragments. It quickly creates thick mats capable of choking boat propellers, making it the primary reason property owners reach out to us.

Water Chestnut

Dense floating clusters that crowd the water's surface and deposit sharp nutlets along shorelines. Prompt action is critical, so we perform mechanical extraction prior to seed release.

Hydrilla

Rapid-spreading and strictly managed throughout New York State. Because it regenerates from underground tubers, ongoing seasonal mechanical harvesting is required to keep it fully under control.

Lily Pads

Dense growths that snare swimmers and immobilize small watercraft. We open up dock areas and navigation channels while preserving designated sections for aquatic life upon request.

Cattails

Dense, deeply rooted growth requires specialized equipment. Rather than simple cutting, we excavate the full root system to prevent further expansion across your waterbody.

Algae & Duckweed

Floating film that gathers along shoreline areas. We skim and extract it from the water before it darkens your waterfront.
